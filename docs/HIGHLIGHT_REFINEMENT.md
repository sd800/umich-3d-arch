# UMich highlight refinement

All 29 architectural highlights are individually reviewed in [the architectural form audit](HIGHLIGHT_ARCHITECTURE_AUDIT.md). That audit records source-specific reconstructions and the individual forms retained after review. The component table below documents joinery only; a frame count is not architectural verification. This remains a public-source exterior interpretation; opening positions and member dimensions are not a measured facade survey.

Shaped reveals follow the existing glass outlines, including pointed Gothic heads. Frames and transoms remain within each opening zone. Coping follows actual roof triangles, including concave plans, and stays supported on its roof finish. No automatic rooftop equipment boxes or arbitrary full-height facade rods are introduced. Repeated components retain separate material/feature ownership.

Kahn Pavilion receives deeper light precast reveals, informed by the architect's photographed three-dimensional facade panels. Burton and Lurie carillons have hollow flared bell shells rather than solid cone placeholders. Bell arrangements and sizes are approximate. The current Burton facility page states 53 bells, while some other university descriptions state 55; this model does not claim an instrument survey.
