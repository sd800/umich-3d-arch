## Additional photos checked: Bursley and Markley

- **Bursley / 1000555 (M357)**: university exterior photos show low common-area ranges with **flat roofs**, broad horizontal glazing, warm golden-brown textured wall/spandrel fields, and rough multicolored fieldstone retaining/base walls at the sloping landscaped court. One low wing has a glazed upper band over an open/recessed ground arcade with pale piers. Behind it are taller housing ranges with horizontal strip glazing. Color is clearly warmer/golder than Angell/Rackham pale limestone. These photos do not verify the existing seven-storey overall count or the material composition of the textured wall. Keep low commons separate from taller housing wings; no invented slate/gable roof. Photos: https://tour.umich.edu/uploads/bursley-spring-2-copy.jpg ; https://tour.umich.edu/uploads/bursley-spring-7.jpg . Primary tour https://tour.umich.edu/text-based/locations/bursley-hall/
- **Mary Markley / 1000060 (M011)**: university photos show **flat roofs**, red brick, dark gray window frames, horizontal pale concrete/stone projecting slabs/bands on lower ranges, taller residence blocks with vertically stacked paired/triple windows, blank projecting brick stair/service bays, and a set-back upper louver/service strip. Terrain makes ground/base exposure vary. One service-side view shows ground windows plus five regular window tiers and a top louver strip; low street-facing ranges show three principal tiers above an exposed basement. Therefore existing five levels must not be spread uniformly over all connected pieces. No pitched roof supported. Photos: https://tour.umich.edu/uploads/markley-fall-38.jpg ; https://tour.umich.edu/uploads/markley-fall-21-1-.jpg ; https://tour.umich.edu/uploads/markley-spring-2-copy.jpg . Primary tour https://tour.umich.edu/text-based/locations/mary-markley-hall/

## Tappan primary photos checked

- **Tappan / 1000216 (M100)**: new map-gallery angle **XP2S1701 clearly shows a shallow gray HIP above the large-arched brick addition**. This supersedes an earlier inference of a flat edge from the oblique Student Life image, whose crop obscured the slopes. Addition walls are red brick with a pale horizontally coursed base and very large inset semicircular brick arch with dark peripheral glazing. Original historic front is also red brick, with a rough pale stone base, two tall window tiers, recessed round brick entry arch, projecting brick piers/bays, and broad pale eaves. Exact roof of every original wing remains obscured; do not generate a measured ridge network. Use the shallow hip as photo-supported for the clear large-arch mass, and retain original/addition facade differences. Actual inspected primary images: https://mapproxy.studentlife.umich.edu/images/tappan-hall/XP2S1701.jpg (clear hip), https://mapproxy.studentlife.umich.edu/images/tappan-hall/XP2S1691.jpg (original front), https://mapproxy.studentlife.umich.edu/images/tappan-hall/XP2S1694.jpg (original entry); gallery https://maps.studentlife.umich.edu/building/tappan-hall . Two visible principal facade tiers do not authenticate the total floors including basement/attic.
